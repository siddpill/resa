# Renewable Energy Services Architecture (RESA)

**Doctoral research initiative | Industry engagement and research collaboration**

> **Research status:** Ongoing doctoral research. RESA is a proposed research concept, not a validated or commercially available solution. The public materials in this repository are intentionally limited to protect unpublished doctoral work and research participants.

## Overview

As electricity systems incorporate more variable renewable generation, distributed energy resources, storage, and digital services, coordination across organizations and technologies becomes increasingly important. Interoperability, operational flexibility, reliability, and governance present practical challenges that merit systematic investigation.

**Renewable Energy Services Architecture (RESA)** is the working name for my doctoral research exploring whether and how service-oriented architectural principles could help address coordination and interoperability challenges in renewable-energy ecosystems.

This repository is a **public research overview and professional collaboration point**. It is not the dissertation manuscript, a publication of original findings, or a source of production-ready energy-system designs.
